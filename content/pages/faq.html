A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Ask us directly ↗

THE PRODUCT

Design & specifications

Can I customize a design for my brand?

Yes. Share your references and the details you want to explore, such as shape, length, color, finish and decoration. We’ll discuss what can be developed for your project.

Can I start from an existing style?

Yes. You can ask about a style in our collections and discuss branding or presentation options for it.

What can I include in a set?

Set contents and application components are confirmed as part of the product specification. Include your preferences in the enquiry.

YOUR PROJECT

Orders & quotations

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum quantities depend on the style and specification. Tell us what you are considering and we’ll confirm the applicable minimum in your quotation.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on the product details, materials, set contents, packaging and order quantity. We provide a project-specific quotation after reviewing your brief.

How long do sampling and production take?

Timing depends on the product scope and current production plan. We’ll confirm sample and production timing when we review your requirements.

PRESENTATION & DELIVERY

Packaging & practical details

Can you help with custom packaging?

Packaging and product presentation can be discussed alongside your design. Include your target format, artwork direction and retail needs in your brief.

Which markets do you work with?

Sevenail works with customers in global markets. Share your destination and any market-specific requirements so we can discuss the project scope.

Can you share audit or product documentation?

Tell us which requirements matter to your team. We can discuss the relevant documentation and audit information for the project.
