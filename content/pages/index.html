About Sevenail

20 Years. One Focus:
Your Brand's Nails.

Based in China with a 2,800m² facility, Sevenail manufactures press-on nails for brands across the US, Europe, and beyond — from concept to certified, shelf-ready product.

  • Disney FAMA authorised manufacturer
  • SMETA ethical trade audited by Intertek
  • ISO GMP certified facility · Bureau Veritas
  • 5,000 MOQ · 20-working-day production lead time
