# Motion study

Reference cues studied from Awwwards element videos:

- [Corporate Fashion Industries — hero](https://assets.awwwards.com/awards/element/2026/01/697b52e70cedc470759650.mp4): editorial split composition; image and headline arrive in a short sequence, then supporting copy follows.
- [Corporate Fashion Industries — detail sidebar](https://assets.awwwards.com/awards/element/2026/01/697b54dc6a720891908021.mp4): image and text panels change in a restrained sequence.
- [Estudio Niksen — product grid](https://assets.awwwards.com/awards/element/2026/09/6abc20597a5e0954032817.mp4): image-led product browsing with a calm, consistent rhythm.
- [Estudio Niksen — navigation](https://assets.awwwards.com/awards/element/2026/09/6abc25832e56e980767607.mp4) and [product detail](https://assets.awwwards.com/awards/element/2026/09/6abc2585d39eb091634348.mp4): navigation and detail interactions stay quiet around product imagery.

Applied across Sevenail: sequenced hero entrance, viewport-triggered section and card reveals, product image reveal and hover zoom, thumbnail and option feedback, navigation and button feedback, and FAQ answer transitions. Timing and easing are adapted to Sevenail’s existing editorial layout and palette. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables movement and reveals content immediately.

The Awwwards motion census could not run because its optional Playwright dependency was unavailable. The patterns above were selected by reviewing the published reference videos; the original video assets are not part of the site.
